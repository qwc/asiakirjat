Manage Robot Users

{{template "admin_nav" "robots"}}

A robot is an ordinary account that signs in with a token instead of a password. The token is the credential; the robot's username is the subject — what you grant access to, and what appears as the uploader of every version it pushes.

What a robot may reach is its grants, exactly as for a person. A token scoped to one project narrows that further; it never widens it. Several tokens on one robot are several credentials for the same identity — issue one per pipeline and revoke it on its own.
