Access Groups

{{template "admin_nav" "access-groups"}}

A group is a named set of people — individual users, LDAP groups, OAuth2 groups, or any mix. A group grants nothing on its own: you give it a role on a project or an organization, and the same group can be an editor on one and a viewer on another.

A member naming a user takes effect immediately. A member naming an LDAP or OAuth2 group reaches each person at their next sign-in, because group membership is only known while they authenticate.
